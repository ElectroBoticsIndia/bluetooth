iPhone and iPad cannot do this in Safari. Apple makes every iOS browser use WebKit, which has no Bluetooth or serial access — so Chrome and Edge on iOS are blocked too. Open this page in Bluefy or WebBLE instead (free on the App Store, they ship their own Bluetooth stack) and BLE will work normally.
